What counts as a favourable rate?

Bank of Canada: a currency is marked favourable when the amount bought per CAD is within 0.5% of its best rate over the past 90 calendar days, or at least 2% above its preceding 30-day average. A higher amount received per CAD is better.

VBCE: choose a target amount of foreign currency per CAD in Alerts. A fresh cash quote at or above your target qualifies. Cash estimates use the VBCE selling price; confirm your actual rate with VBCE. Charts and history always show Bank of Canada reference data.

This compares recent rates; it does not predict what happens next. Data more than four calendar days old is marked stale and cannot trigger a favourable-rate signal.

Publication is scheduled hourly from 9:15 AM to 5:15 PM Vancouver time, every day. Publishing can be delayed. Holidays retain the last available fixing. What counts as favourable

Bank of Canada: the same published rate never alerts twice. A new fixing can alert sooner if it improves another 1%. Data more than four days old cannot trigger an alert.
